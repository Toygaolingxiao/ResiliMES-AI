ResiliMES-AI01 · Problemuser: fab scheduling engineer / shift lead

The user

A fab shift lead has to trade delivery, Q-time and stability every hour, and has to explain each call.

Q-time windows

A dataset CQT window (e.g. dry etch → wet etch) runs 1–24 h. A missed wet-etch window sends the lot back for a re-clean.

Disruptions

Scanners break, PMs land in the peak, hot lots arrive. The dispatch rule has no notion of "why".

Plan stability

A lot already sent to a tool's port should not be re-planned and moved twice.

Explanations

Next shift asks "why did this family's WIP double?" and "why is this lot late?". Today that is hours of log reading.

What the shift lead needs: a schedule that respects the fab's rules, honest numbers on what each rule costs, and evidence-backed answers to "why", without handing the wafer to a language model.
Not a chatbot. The language model never places a lot. It reads evidence, picks among engine-built legal options, and every number it writes is checked.

Q-time range: docs/QTIME_URGENT.md · frozen-window rule: docs/SCHED_FREEZE.md · AI scope: docs/AI_SHIFT_LEAD.md, docs/AI_INVESTIGATOR.md

ResiliMES-AI02 · Solutiondeterministic core · advisory AI

What we built

Three time layers decide. The AI explains and advises.

Decides · rule-based, tested
1 · Planning (daily)Backward pass over SMT2020 standard times + forward litho capacity → step dues and baseline priority. default
2 · Scheduling tick (5 min)Short-term state: queues, up/down/PM, open Q-time windows, batches, setups, transport.
3 · Dispatch (event)Start the scheduled lot or deviate and log the reason. Hard Q-time gate on the whole CQT segment, on by default; optional frozen window.
Advises · Nemotron via Nightshift
Investigator"Why did WIP of family F rise?" ≤ 6 evidence tool calls → one cause from a fixed taxonomy.
AI shift leadExceptions only: rules first, no-AI twin preview; called only when it projects an avoidable Q-time violation or a late hot lot.
Handover · Late-lot explainer · H-MEM12 h shift report, exact lateness split + one verified counterfactual, decision memory.

ResiliMES-AI04 · Fab realismpublic benchmark, not a production recording

Fab realism

The full SMT2020 fab, with the rules a real fab runs on.

SMT2020 LV/HM
1,313 tools

106 families, 102 litho tools, 10 products, 2,156 WIP lots, 10 routes of 242–583 steps. Published MTTF/MTTR and PM calendars.

Q-time
264 CQT limits

Windows of 1–24 h. Q-time-urgent override with a fixed 15 min margin. Whole-segment hard gate with the planning layer, on by default.

Frozen dispatch window
2 loads / 30 min

Each tool keeps its running lot plus its next loads fixed at every 300 s tick. Leaves only by a logged exception (down, PM, Q-time, hot lot…).

Lot transport
1 / 3 min

Same station group / other group, all policies. Q-time keeps running during the move. Example 2-building layout: 15 min cross-building (off by default).

Honest about the data. SMT2020 names no bays; the 1/3 min values and the frozen-window limits are the fab engineer's rules, fixed before any run and labelled as such.
Checked. make smt-invariants replays every start of every book and policy: all 15 checks are 0. Seeds, dues and weights were never tuned.

Program-enforced bounds
  • Call budget 6; repeat call → stop
  • Fact-check + one repair on every cited number and id
  • Degraded route (縮退): fact-check fail, empty answer, provider error or timeout → rule result, labelled with the reason
  • Time budgets: 90 s per call (TTFB header), 180 s per task; timeout → "timeout"
  • Reasoning tokens count in max_tokens: 502 empty → 4k → 8k → 16k
  • Advice only: never moves a lot
Per-round trace: decision · evidence · stop_reason = answered | budget_exhausted | repeat_call | fact_check_repaired | degraded_to_rule | error
Design principles: rules first, AI for exceptions and explanation · every AI claim fact-checked or simulator-verified · Q-time is a hard constraint.

Honest fallbacks, always labelled. No Nightshift token: scripted stand-in, badge "offline stand-in", no request id. No Zetaris key: the query line says "local extract (Zetaris not called)". Nothing pretends a model or a warehouse answered.
What is unusual. Most agents let the model decide and explain. Here a tested scheduler decides, Scout will not run the loop without a fresh contract, and Nemotron works in the lanes where language helps: evidence, choice among legal options, reports.
